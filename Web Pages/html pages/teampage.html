Our mission

Built by students, for students.

UOPlanner is created by software engineering and computer science students at the University of Ottawa who want to make a common academic planning challenge easier. Our team brings together technical development, product thinking, and user experience to turn a stressful task into a clearer, more manageable process. We believe a better-planned week can help students make room for their studies and their lives.
